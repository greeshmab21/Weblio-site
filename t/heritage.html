How a restoration unfolds

i.

Assessment

We go up, count broken tiles, check underlayment and decking, and tell you honestly whether you need a restoration or only repairs.

ii.

Sourcing

We match your tile or slate from salvage yards and quarries, and hold samples against your roof before ordering.

iii.

Restoration

Original pieces are numbered, lifted and relaid by hand. Most homes take two to four weeks.

Begin with a consultation

A roof assessment with written findings and photographs is $150, credited toward your project if you proceed.
