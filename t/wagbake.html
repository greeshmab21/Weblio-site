      Good questions

      Ask us anything.

      How long do the treats stay fresh?+

      Crunchy treats keep for about three weeks in an airtight jar. Chewy ones and cakes are best within a week, kept in the fridge. Every bag has a baked-on date.

      My dog has allergies. Can you help?+

      Yes. Use the filters to find grain-free or low-fat treats, and tell us about allergies when you order. We can bake most recipes without a specific ingredient.

      Are they safe for puppies?+

      Treats marked "Puppy safe" are soft enough and gentle enough for pups over 12 weeks. Treats are a snack, so keep them to a small part of their day.

      How do I order and pay?+

      Build your box or cake here, then send it to us on WhatsApp or Instagram. We'll confirm the total, delivery or pickup time, and send a payment link.

      Do you deliver?+

      We deliver locally twice a week and offer pickup from the bakery. Message us your area and we'll let you know the next delivery day.

      Who's a good boy?

      Yours is. Treat them to something baked fresh this week.
