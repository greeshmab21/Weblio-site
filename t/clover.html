Anatomy of a Clover cookie

Take a bite.

Crackly golden edge

Brown butter, two sugars and a 48-hour dough rest. That's where the crunch comes from.

Gooey, barely-set center

Baked hot and fast so the middle stays soft for hours. Pull it apart. It stretches.

Puddles of chocolate

Chopped bars, not chips, so you get melty pools and little shards in every bite.

A pinch of flaky salt

Finished by hand right out of the oven. Sweet, then salty, then you want another.
