Good
questions

Do I need photos or a logo first?

No. We can work from your Instagram posts and phone photos, and design a simple logo if you don't have one.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain and the site are registered in your name, and you can take them anywhere.

Can I make changes myself?

Yes. Framer sites have a simple editor for text and photos, and we'll walk you through it on a short call after launch.

You're not local. Does that matter?

Not really. We work with businesses across the US over email, text and video calls, and schedule calls around your working hours.

What does the free mockup include?

A designed homepage for your business with your name, services and real photos where we have them. If you don't love it, you owe nothing.
